### 5. Depression: Screening and Diagnosis

- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30277728/

Women should be screened for depression at least once during the perinatal period using the PHQ-2,
PHQ-9, or Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale. In older adults, the Geriatric Depression Scale is
also an appropriate screening tool for depression.
